# Guide and URL Contract

The catalog links to `/<bridge>/` on `https://open-mqtt-bridges.github.io/`. Preserve old page URLs and tracked anchors in place or through tested compatibility pages. Keep stable paths when changing navigation labels. The IDM Energy page identifies its archived status and links to its historical repository.

Each active guide uses the same navigation order:

1. **Overview**: What the bridge does, a practical example, what it does not do, and the next action.
2. **Getting started**: Prerequisites, recommended setup, expected output, and a quick verification.
3. **Configuration**: Required settings first, safe examples, and guidance on credentials.
4. **Using the bridge**: Typical tasks and how to recognize success.
5. **Troubleshooting**: Symptom, check, and next action.
6. **Reference**: MQTT topics and payloads, compatibility, and links to separate contributor details where relevant.

Use plain English, define MQTT and broker where relevant, and keep exact settings and commands. Do not invent features for consistency. Readers can return to the central catalog from every guide.
